Calendar Updater

A personal tool.

Calendar Updater runs on its owner's own Mac. It reads the owner's email there and proposes changes to the family's Google Calendars: new events, changed times, cancellations. Nothing is written until the owner looks at the list and presses Sync to Google.

It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
