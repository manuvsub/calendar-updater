Privacy policy

Calendar Updater is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Mac.

  • What it accesses: the events on the Google Calendars of the owner's account, the owner's own and the family calendars shared with it, through Google's Calendar API (the calendar.events permission), to read them and to add or change the events the owner approves.
  • Where the data stays: on the owner's Mac. To compare the owner's email, or a file or a message the owner gives the app, with the calendar, event titles, places and the start of their descriptions are sent with it to Anthropic's Claude model, which the owner uses for this.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for anything else. The use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: the owner can remove the app's access at any time in the Google Account (Security → Third-party apps) or in the app itself.
